Privacy & security
  • Never sends for you

    Verbia only drafts replies. It never sends or submits anything for you.

  • Keeps no conversation history

    The context used to draft a reply and the reply itself are not saved as conversation history.

  • Collects no personal information

    Verbia does not collect names, email addresses, or account details from the pages you visit.

  • Access on demand

    Verbia reads a page only when you summon it, or on a site you authorized — and you can manage or revoke that access at any time.
